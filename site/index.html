What repeated

Eight of nine takeaway citation sets point to the same closing paragraph: define a condition that can fail, preserve evidence, and compare the workflow including human work. Most KEEP rationales reiterate the draft’s existing limits.

No supported recurring editorial issue reached the two-of-three within-role review heuristic. That heuristic is not statistical confidence.

What needed caution

All 18 findings and 62 citation references were reviewed by Codex. Revealed-evidence integrity passed; semantic support still needed qualifications.

Some model rationales loosely call rendered comparisons workflow results, or reported observations hypotheses. Preserve the article’s precise wording; do not copy those rationales as verified claims.

05 / VALIDATION

Human accuracy remains open.

Observed in this setup

Mechanics and editorial consistency

Nine live sessions passed the trace audit. The input, conditions, outcomes, clocks and rejected actions are accounted for. A separate integration pilot and offline controls tested the execution path.

The model batch did not identify a useful edit beyond the existing close review. Incremental editorial value remains unproven.

Not measured

Prediction accuracy and uplift

Reader collection was excluded. Completion, interest, reading time and comprehension have no independent human reference. Mismatches are unavailable, not zero.

Calibration stays not_calibrated, confidence intervals null, and coverage unknown. No candidate B was created.

If validation is pursued later, freeze predictions before collecting independent first-time reader outcomes and report individual agreements and mismatches. A small reader diagnostic can expose failures; it cannot certify calibrated probabilities. A 24-reader proposal in the earlier Opus case is historical, not a completed or current study.
