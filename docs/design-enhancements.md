# Design enhancements for bounded editorial decisions

These changes respond to feedback on the earlier harness. The current release improves evidence handling and reporting; human prediction accuracy remains unmeasured.

## Purpose and framing

**Changed presentation and editorial protocol.** Lead with KEEP / EDIT / INCONCLUSIVE and source-supported editorial consequences. KEEP is allowed; source-check a small candidate only when warranted.

This batch adds limited evidence beyond close editorial review. No candidate B was justified.

## Score transparency and calibration

**Changed rubric and reporting.** Shared interest anchors, a metric dictionary and separate model judgments, executor accounting and human measurement. Uncalibrated forecasts are secondary.

No composite engagement score or claim of calibrated completion probability.

## Accuracy validation

**Explicitly unresolved measurement.** Keep reader outcomes and prediction mismatches separate from software audits. Preserve a protocol for later independent reader checks.

No readers were collected in this experiment; accuracy and comprehension remain unmeasured. This is a reporting improvement, not validated prediction accuracy.

## Dwell and non-linear reading

**Existing actions; new read-only summaries.** Retain variable read duration, backtracking, skip, seek and early exit. Add section paths, separate clocks, unique playback coverage and overlap accounting.

Capabilities being available does not mean agents used them. This batch had no backward scrolls or seeks; dwell interpretation stays unknown without supporting evidence.

## Browser failure modes

**Control probes and bounded recovery.** Preflight article controls, add bounded broken-media metadata handling and clean RPC shutdown, retain rejected actions, and separate blockers from abandonments.

Two live policy state errors recovered in the original sessions. Neither exact signature recurred in a second assigned session; no failed run was retried away.

## Variance across agents

**Fixed repeats and scoped context.** Three independent contexts per each of three authored roles; selected-post allowlist prevents unrelated role overrides leaking into requests. Keep every attempt visible.

Same model alias and settings; no explicit seed, matched-seed trial, population weights or interviewed personas. Variation is descriptive.

## Article fidelity and AI edits

**Constrained rich layout and reviewed disposition.** Preserve paired images, tables, external labels, captions and claim boundaries in a frozen controlled article. Review cited feedback before applying any edit.

Source reconciliation happened before inference. Citation integrity does not establish semantic support; KEEP adds no article claim.
